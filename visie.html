Het Algoritme Rekent

Penpath lays a grid of 31 × 36 points over the face, A to AE across and 1 to 36 down. Then it chooses 800 short lines, each time the one line that brings the drawing closest to your photo.

About a second of numpy, light enough for a free server.

Chapter 3 · Onder De Motorkap

Algorithms You Can Hold

The heart of KUNSD is not the website, it is the maths. Every puzzle is an optimisation problem with one extra rule: a person with a pen must be able to follow the answer.

1,116
grid points

31 columns × 36 rows on the Penpath sheet above, named like Zeeslag.

800
lines chosen

Each one picked greedily: the line that removes the most remaining darkness.

11
pen routes

Lines are joined end to end, so you lift your pen as little as possible.

0.5 s
to compute

A sparse matrix of line × pixel keeps it fast, even on Render's free plan.

Read the photoStretch contrast, boost edges, weigh the centre of the face.
List every lineAll pairs of points up to 5 steps apart that skip no point.
Pick, update, repeatLeast-squares gain per line, refreshed only where it changed.
Make it drawableJoin lines into routes and sort them top to bottom.
Print it sharpA lossless PDF from KUNSD's own writer, the answer behind a fold.

Chapter 4 · Speeltuin

No Pen? Speeltuin.

Ready-to-go effects for when you just want a striking picture. One photo, one slider per effect, instant download.

A

Shadow Stripes

Each scan line gets its own darkness threshold, spread out by the golden ratio. Where the photo is darker, a stripe appears. Scroll, or drag the slider, to turn the angle. The preview redraws in milliseconds in your browser.

B

Three More To Play With

Drag the circle grid, let squares drift away from the centre, or rank dots by darkness. Upload once, use it everywhere.
